## Profile

Senior AI & LLMs Engineer with **4+ years** of commercial experience architecting production-grade AI systems and shipping them as SaaS products. I take AI from concept and user stories to live deployment across **Agentic AI, RAG, LLM fine-tuning, voice AI, Computer Vision, NLP, and Generative AI**, with the backend, cloud, and DevOps depth to run it all in production.

My work spans **fully autonomous voice agents**, **HIPAA-compliant clinical-workflow agents running across 15+ U.S. clinics**, and **LLM copilots plugged directly into live ERP platforms**. I also lead: I have managed engineers building agentic AI workflows, and I mentor engineers and non-technical SMEs as a **Principal Technical Trainer**.
